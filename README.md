### Squence

It's always on one day, and the sequence is Listening, Reading, and Writing.

> **Notice** there is a mini break between modules.

After each module (listening, writing, reading), the test center will take your answer sheet and question sheet back from you and give the next one to you.
1 and 1/5 minutes.

### Structure
40 questions and 4 sections
The difficulty level always increases from one section to another.
30 minutes of audio and 10 minutes to transfer answers.
Answer while you listen to the audio

### Headphones
You will be provided with headphones
When you are provided with headphones
  - check if they are working well
  - check the volume level
  - ask them to change it if it's not working
  - you have 10 minutes to find the volume key and change it to your comfort level.
> **Exeption** if all of a sudden, the audio is noisy the have to **Stop** the audio and play it again.

### Breaks
There are 4 sections and a break between sections.
There is a break in sections(question 1,2..6 are a one question)

### Audio
Audio will be played **Once**.
 > **Notice** there are various accents. (British, American, Canadian, etc.
85-90% British Accent.


## Listening module
### Troubles
Two reasons not to get 9 bands
  - Non-native Speakers
  - Not engrossed with the language
> start listening to music **With** lyrics or movies **with** subtitle (spacially UK movies)
### Difficulty level
Difficulty level increases in each section, and this is the criterion by which it gets harder
  - Speed (sections 3 and 4 have almost the same speed)
  - Distraction (for example, the guy says $ 12 at first, then corrects it to 12.5$)
  - Types of questions (fill the gap, multiple choice question, ...)
  - No breaks within sections (at section 4, there is no break from 31 to 40)
> **Why sections?** 4 sections = 4 context
